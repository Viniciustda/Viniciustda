### 🧑‍💻 About me

- 🎓 Computer Science student at the **Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)**.
- 🔬 Undergraduate researcher (CNPq scholar) at the **Wireless Networks Lab (WiNet)** and the **CCSC Research Lab**, UFMG.
- 🛡️ I research the **security of UAV networks (FANETs)**: man-in-the-middle attacks, lightweight cryptography and multi-agent reinforcement learning.
- 🎮 I also enjoy **game development** and **robotics**.
- 🪖 Former Brazilian Army officer (Second Lieutenant), where I led teams and administrative sections.

### 🔬 Research

| Project | Status |
|---|---|
| **[KEYSUAV](https://github.com/Viniciustda/KEYSUAV)**: an opportunistic key exchange scheme (with the lightweight cipher ASCON) that protects location sharing in UAV networks against man-in-the-middle attacks. | Published at **SBRC 2026** ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.5753/sbrc.2026.19890)) |
| **CIRRUS**: detection and identification of man-in-the-middle attackers in location sharing for FANETs with multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL). | Ongoing |
| **MDPSPR**: multisource dual-path same-parity routing for dual-radio wireless sensor networks, evaluated in ns-3. | Ongoing |
| **Undergraduate thesis**: MitM detection and identification in local consensus services for FANETs with MARL. | Ongoing |
